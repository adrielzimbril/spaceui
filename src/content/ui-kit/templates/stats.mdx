## Overview

The **Stats Dashboard** template gathers 5 modular statistics blocks into a cohesive portfolio analytics dashboard:

1. **General Metrics (`block-stats-general`)**: High-level KPI overview (Total Views, Coffee Consumed, Site Age).
2. **Reader Engagement (`block-stats-engagement`)**: Audience metrics (Most Viewed & Reacted Content, Emoji Reactions, Topic Tags, Changelog Milestones).
3. **Publication & Content (`block-stats-blog`)**: Authoring analytics (Total Articles, Word Counts, Community Messages, Estimated Reading Time).
4. **GitHub Activity (`block-stats-github`)**: Open-source momentum (Stars, Forks, Commits, 52-Week Contribution Matrix).
5. **Performance Benchmarks (`block-stats-performance`)**: Synthetic Lighthouse audits (Mobile & Desktop Scores with Radar Backgrounds and Progress Bars).
